# BPMN 2.0.2 notation checklist

Every notation element, marker, DI attribute and modeling rule of
[OMG BPMN 2.0.2](https://www.omg.org/spec/BPMN/2.0.2/PDF) (formal/13-12-09)
that the audit found, with its status in BPMN Flow. Page numbers are PDF pages.
Details of each depiction are in [bpmn-support.md](bpmn-support.md); the rules
the editor enforces and the remaining gaps are in
[spec-conformance.md](spec-conformance.md).

- **full**: drawn as the spec shows it and creatable / editable from the UI (rules: enforced or reported).
- **partial**: drawn correctly, but part of the behaviour is missing (see note).
- **known gap**: not supported; the reason is given.
